# Coach Brief

## Positioning

**Leadership Coach — Clarity & Decision-Making Under Pressure**
*Helping leaders grow without burning out*

---

## The Problem She Solves

Most clients don't look burned out. They're still delivering, still making decisions, still growing. But growth has started costing too much — energy, focus, clarity, and eventually the ability to make the right call at the right time.

From the outside: results, responsibility, momentum.
From the inside: sustained pressure, decision fatigue, and shrinking clarity.

The real problem is rarely visible at the surface.

---

## Who She Works With

Leaders and senior experts who are still performing — but already paying too high a price for it. People in international companies where pressure is constant, stakes are high, and "slow down" is not a real option.

Past and current clients include leaders from **Amazon, Google, BMW, Booking, Vodafone, Facebook, Swedbank**, and other international organizations.

---

## What She Does

She helps leaders regain decision-making capacity and clarity — before pressure turns into something that can't be ignored.

The work is structured, precise, and grounded in real business constraints. No generic frameworks. No surface-level motivation. The focus is on seeing the problem clearly first — because the right solution follows from that.

This is 1:1 coaching only.
